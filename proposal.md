# Proposal

## Description

I will be making a grid style time management cooking game,
 where the player/players must prep, cook, and deliver food to customers before the timer runs out.


## Needs to have List

- Grid kitchen with smooth movement and stations you can interact with to perform different tasks.
- Being able to combine ingredients to form foods.
- Title screen, new round screen(aswell as multiple rounds), highscore, score.
- Timer on the round aswell as a progress timer on the foods.
- Order queue system, where the faster you make the order, the more points you get and vice versa.
- A base chef, kitchen items, countertops, sprites. Aswell as sound effects.

## Nice to have List

- Co-Op support, whether its with p5party or on the same device.
- Multiple kitchen maps increasing in difficulty
- Ability to customize chefs/characters/better animations
- Rush hour, where all orders get double the score (if you pass a certain round)
- VIP orders, which have less time but give more score
